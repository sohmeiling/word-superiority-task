Instructions

  1. When you see Ready, look at the + and press Space or tap Ready.
  2. Four letters or a single letter will flash briefly.
  3. A mask and two numbered choices will appear. Their column shows which position to report.
  4. Press 1 or 2, or select a choice. Take as much time as you need and guess if you are unsure.

Example. If you saw LAMP and the choices in the first column were 1 L and 2 D, choose 1 L.

Letter identification accuracy

The word superiority effect is the tendency to identify a briefly presented letter more accurately when it appears in a word than when it appears alone. Compare your word and single-letter scores. The nonword condition shows how you performed when the letter appeared in a scrambled string.

Each condition has 16 trials, so scores can vary from person to person. A class average gives a more stable comparison.
